Admission Process

Your Journey at Plato’s Academy Begins Here

  • Explore our website or contact the admissions office for guidance.
  • Learn about our academics, values and facilities before applying.
  • Fill out the application form online or collect it from the school campus.
  • Provide required documents like birth certificate and academic records.
  • Complete the application fee via online or offline modes.
  • Experience the school atmosphere with a guided visit, if desired.
  • Attend an interaction or entrance test as part of the process.
  • Receive timely updates from our admissions team.
  • On selection, submit original documents and pay fees.
  • Get welcomed with orientation details to begin your journey.
  • Step into a nurturing environment focused on growth, learning and excellence.
  • Attend the parent-student orientation before the academic year begins.

Whether you are seeking a strong academic foundation, competitive exam preparation, or holistic development for your child, our admission team is here to guide you every step of the way. We believe in creating a smooth, transparent admission process where parents and students feel informed, supported and confident.
