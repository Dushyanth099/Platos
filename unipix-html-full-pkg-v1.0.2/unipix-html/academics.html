Our Academics

Plato’s Academy delivers a transformative educational experience, blending ICSE, CBSE, Cambridge, Oxford, and STEM frameworks to foster intellectual agility, innovation, and global competence. Our rigorous curriculum is supported by distinguished faculty, personalized attention, and a commitment to holistic development—ensuring students excel academically and beyond.

Academics Curriculum

Pre-Primary (SPARK Program)

Igniting curiosity through play, foundational skill-building, and character education.

Literacy & numeracy Creative expression Social-emotional development

Grade 1 to 10

Balanced blend of international and national curricula (Cambridge/ICSE/CBSE).

Core subjects (Math, Science, Languages) STEM labs & project-based learning Second languages: French, Hindi, Tamil

Grade 11 & 12

Stream-specific specialization with university-ready academics and coaching.

Competitive exam coaching (NEET/JEE/UPSC/CA) Research and leadership opportunities Stream-based curriculum: Science, Commerce, Humanities
