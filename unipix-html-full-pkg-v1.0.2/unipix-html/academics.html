Our Academics

Plato’s Academy delivers a transformative educational experience, blending ICSE, CBSE, Cambridge, Oxford, and STEM frameworks to foster intellectual agility, innovation, and global competence. Our rigorous curriculum is supported by distinguished faculty, personalized attention, and a commitment to holistic development—ensuring students excel academically and beyond.

Academics Curriculum

Pre-Primary (SPARK Program)

Igniting curiosity through play, foundational skill-building, and character education.

  • ✔ Literacy & numeracy
  • ✔ Creative expression
  • ✔ Social-emotional development
Grade 1 to 10

Balanced blend of international and national curricula (Cambridge/ICSE/CBSE).

  • ✔ Core subjects (Math, Science, Languages)
  • ✔ STEM labs & project-based learning
  • ✔ Second languages: French, Hindi, Tamil
Grade 11 & 12

Stream-specific specialization with university-ready academics and coaching.

  • ✔ Competitive exam coaching (NEET/JEE/UPSC/CA)
  • ✔ Research and leadership opportunities
  • ✔ Stream-based curriculum: Science, Commerce, Humanities

Staff

Roles

  • Deliver high-impact, student-centric pedagogy.
  • Mentor students in academics and co-curriculars.
  • Collaborate on curriculum innovation and safety protocols.
  • Support students' emotional well-being and personal growth.
  • Engage in ongoing professional development and training.

Roles

Students

  • Uphold academic integrity and active participation.
  • Engage in leadership, service, and extracurriculars.
  • Adhere to school values and disciplinary guidelines.
  • Foster a respectful and inclusive school community.
  • Take initiative in learning and personal development.
