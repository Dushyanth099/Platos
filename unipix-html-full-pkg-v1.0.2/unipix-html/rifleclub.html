Rifle Club

Plato's Academy Rifle Club🏅

Towards International Success

We are strategically building a pathway to international representation by aligning our training standards with Olympic protocols and integrating sports psychology, nutrition, and biomechanics into our regimen. Our goal is not just to compete but to dominate the global shooting arena.

VISION

To become a premier institution in India for competitive shooting, renowned for producing internationally acclaimed shooters who embody excellence, integrity, and resilience.

MISSION

To develop confident, focused, and ethically grounded marksmen and markswomen by offering elite-level coaching, exposure to high-pressure competitions, and an environment that fosters both individual growth and team spirit.

Watch Video

From state-level podiums to national championship finals, our athletes have consistently demonstrated exemplary skill and sportsmanship. Through structured coaching, simulation-based drills, and exposure to competitive circuits, we are grooming the next generation of shooting stars, primed for international glory.

Where Precision Meets Purpose

Plato's Academy Rifle Club stands as a beacon of discipline, precision, and performance in the field of marksmanship. Founded with the vision of nurturing young talent and building champions, the club offers world-class training in air rifle and small bore shooting disciplines. With professional coaches, modern infrastructure, and a curriculum that balances technical skill with mental focus, the club empowers students to reach new heights in competitive shooting.
