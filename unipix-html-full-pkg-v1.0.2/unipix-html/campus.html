Campus

Campus resources

Life at Plato’s Academy is vibrant and enriching. Students engage in a plethora of activities, from literary clubs and science symposiums to music ensembles and art workshops. Our annual 'Global Week' celebrates cultural diversity, promoting inclusivity and global awareness. The campus buzzes with energy, fostering a sense of community and belonging among students.

Smart Class Roooms

Interactive digital tools make learning more engaging and effective.

Library

A rich collection of books and resources to spark curiosity.

Auditorium

A vibrant space for events, performances and presentations.

Ventilated Classrooms

Bright, airy classrooms for a healthy learning environment.

Well-furnished Laboratory

Modern labs designed for safe and hands-on experiments.

Playground

Spacious grounds for sports, fun and fitness.

Amenities

Cricket Ground

A full-sized pitch to nurture budding cricket talents.

Football ground

Well-maintained turf for energetic football practice and matches.

Tennis Ground

Open courts that promote teamwork, agility and fun.

Basketball Ground

Skating

Athletic Tracks

Rifle Club

Safe and supervised setup for precision shooting practice.

Mutlipurpose Hall

Versatile indoor space for sports, events and activities.

Campus Safety

24/7 surveillance ensures a safe and secure campus environment.

Front Gate Security

Strict entry checks and monitoring for student safety at all times.

Vigilant Mentors

Counselling and guidance Arena

A safe space offering emotional support and career guidance.

Medical Attention

On-call medical care and first aid for quick emergency response.

Transport

Well-maintained Vehicle

In & Around Transport Facility

Individual Pickup Point

Houses

🏛️

Aristotle House

Aristotle House symbolizes courage, passion and determination. The color red represents the fire within, igniting ambition and inspiring action. Known for its fearless spirit, the Red House never backs down in the face of challenges. With unwavering commitment and boundless energy, they set high standards in every endeavor. Their leadership and resilience make them a driving force in both academics and sports.

🏛️

Darwin House

Darwin House represents energy, positivity and enlightenment. The color yellow shines like the sun, spreading warmth and joy. The Yellow House is known for creativity and resilience, always radiating optimism. Their vibrant enthusiasm and innovative ideas bring life to every event. Their unity and spirit make them a pillar of inspiration across the school.

🏛️

Einstein House

Einstein House stands for growth, harmony and balance. The color green reflects the essence of nature, reminding us of renewal and fresh beginnings. This house is celebrated for its teamwork and sportsmanship, always striving for greatness. Their calm confidence and collective strength help them evolve through every challenge. Einstein House consistently demonstrates dedication and a love for learning.

🏛️

Newton House

Newton House embodies peace, stability and perseverance. The color blue signifies depth and wisdom, symbolizing calmness under pressure and strength in adversity. Renowned for their focus and discipline, the Blue House remains steadfast in all pursuits. Their silent determination and thoughtful strategies make them consistent achievers. Newton House reflects a spirit of excellence, commitment and balance.
